Profile

I'm a biomedical engineer and PhD researcher (Wayne State University) working at the intersection of gait analysis, wearable sensing, and neurorehabilitation. My research focuses on developing low-cost, deployable tools — radar, wearable sensors, and motion-capture-driven feedback systems — for fall-risk assessment and movement recovery after stroke or neuromuscular injury.

I've led and contributed to peer-reviewed studies published in IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering and the Journal of Biomechanics Open, supported by government-funded research grants and competitive fellowships. Alongside my research, I mentor undergraduate researchers and K-12 students through STEM outreach, and I care about building tools that hold up outside the lab — in real clinics, with real patients.
