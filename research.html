Research

Publications
An Open-Source System for Real-Time Visual Feedback of Kinematic Data During Rehabilitation
First author · Journal of Biomechanics Open (Elsevier), 2026
An open-source system that extracts marker data from Vicon Nexus in real time and streams live joint kinematics to third-party platforms like Unity, closing the gap between gold-standard motion capture and real-time patient feedback. Validated on 28 healthy adults, with strong agreement to standard motion capture at the hip and knee.
mmWave Radar for Sit-to-Stand Analysis: A Comparative Study With Wearables and Kinect
Co-author · IEEE Transactions on Biomedical Engineering, 2025
A 45-participant study applying 60 GHz radar as a non-contact, privacy-preserving alternative to wearable and camera-based motion capture for sit-to-stand assessment — a core clinical measure of fall risk.
